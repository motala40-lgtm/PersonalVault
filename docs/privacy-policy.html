Privacy Policy for Easy Archive

Last updated: August 2026

Easy Archive ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

1. Information collection

Easy Archive does not collect, upload to any cloud, or transmit any personal information. All your data (photos, files, notes, contacts, passwords) is stored solely and locally on your own device.

2. Permissions

The app may request access to the camera (for document scanning), storage (to add and save files), and notifications (for reminders). None of these permissions are used to send any information anywhere.

3. Third-party sharing

No data is shared with any third party, because no data ever leaves your device.

4. Internet

The app does not require internet access for its core functionality.

5. Crash reports

If the app closes unexpectedly, you may optionally choose to send a technical crash report (device model, Android version, and error details only — never your vault's contents) via your own email app. This is never sent automatically.

6. Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact us at: Newlifetech25@hotmail.com
